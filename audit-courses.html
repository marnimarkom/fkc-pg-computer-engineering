Audit Courses

Who must enrol?

Under FKC admission guidance, admitted students from Computer Science or other non-engineering backgrounds with prescribed engineering knowledge gaps must enrol in the assigned audit courses. Admission is subject to academic evaluation.

Registration and completion

  1. Check the admission conditions and prescribed courses with FKC.
  2. Confirm the registration procedure and course availability with the faculty.
  3. Register and fulfil the prescribed course assessment requirements.

Confirm with FKC whether courses must be completed before Proposal Defence and how prior bachelor’s and master’s qualifications affect audit requirements.
