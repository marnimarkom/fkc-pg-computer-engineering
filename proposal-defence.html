Proposal Defence

ME20 and PE20 · Research candidature milestone

Eligibility and preparation

  • Maintain active registration.
  • Complete Research Methodology or obtain approved exemption. FKC expects a pass before defence.
  • Check prescribed audit course requirements with FKC; the deadline relative to defence is pending confirmation.
  • Prepare a research proposal and presentation slides; obtain main supervisor approval.
  • Research title: maximum 15 words unless an exception is approved.

Calculated from initial registration. These are defence dates, not application deadlines. Apply in advance for panel arrangements.

Overdue or unsuccessful defence

Under Sections 8.7.3–8.7.6, the faculty issues two warning letters after the stipulated period; termination may follow. The regulations require termination for students who have not undergone defence and exceed 24 months (MSc) or 36 months (PhD) from initial registration. Students who fail are given an interval of 3 months (MSc) or 6 months (PhD) to repeat the defence.

Application

Prepare the proposal, course completion or exemption evidence, and any panel information requested by FKC. The main supervisor submits the application.
