Completion and exemption

Section 8.7.5 requires completion before Proposal Defence for students registered from October 2020. Section 10.3 requires a pass before thesis submission. FKC expects students to pass before defence. Students who took the course at master’s level may apply to the faculty for exemption during the first semester, subject to conditions (Section 7.2.2).

Reference: UniMAP Academic Regulations for Postgraduate Research Programs (2025), CGS forms and guidelines.
