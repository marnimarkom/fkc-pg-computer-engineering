Supervisor selection

MSc by Research (ME20)

1 main supervisor + 1 co-supervisor

PhD by Research (PE20)

1 main supervisor + 2 co-supervisors

How to identify a potential supervisor

  1. Explore the research domains and supervisor directory.
  2. Identify staff whose expertise aligns with your proposed research.
  3. Prepare a brief research proposal and academic background.
  4. Contact the potential main supervisor to discuss research fit and availability.
  5. Confirm the proposed supervision team through the formal university process.

Co-supervisors from other universities may be proposed, subject to eligibility and approval. Confirm arrangements for Industrial PhD and PhD by Publication separately.
